# General Writing Rules

## Voice
- Trustworthy — never overstate, never trash-talk, never promise what isn't real
- Authoritative — specifics over abstractions, show the receipts
- Humor — dry, understated, nerdy; wit from specifics, not jokes about the topic

## Phrases and tells to avoid
- Em dashes. Use commas, parentheses, or two sentences. Strongest AI tell.
- "Delve", "tapestry", "ever-evolving", "in today's fast-paced world"
- "It's important to note that..."
- "Not just X, but Y" construction
- "Let's unpack this", "let's dive in"
- Openings like "I've been thinking about..."
- LinkedIn clichés: "humbled to announce", "thrilled to share"
- Corporate filler: "leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem" as buzzwords

## Voice rules
- First person singular ("I"). No "we at Addition..." yet.
- Specifics beat generalities. Name the exact arm, model, observation.
- If speculating, say so.
- Don't moralize. Don't lecture.

## Punctuation / habits
- No em dashes
- No excessive ellipses
- Emojis sparingly (at most one per post, only if it adds signal)
