# X Articles (long-form) Rules

## Length
- 800–2,500 words standard; longer for heavy build logs

## Title
- Concrete and specific. "What broke when I trained a pi-0.5 policy on the
  OpenArm" beats "Lessons from training a policy"
- No clickbait hooks

## Opening
- First line grounds the reader in a specific scene, number, or claim
- No "in today's rapidly evolving world" openings
- No throat-clearing

## Structure
- Short section headers
- Short paragraphs; break up dense prose
- Generous with specifics, code, configs

## Formatting
- Plain markdown, minimal styling (X strips most of it)
- No em dashes
- Links inline

## Close
- Specific observation or open question. No summary. No "thanks for reading".
