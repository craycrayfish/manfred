# LinkedIn Rules

## Length
- 100–300 words sweet spot; up to ~500 when the story earns it

## Hashtags
- 1–3 at the bottom. Not sprinkled throughout.

## Formatting
- Short paragraphs, whitespace between them
- One sentence per line when the sentence carries weight
- Avoid walls of text

## Default structure
1. Hook (1–2 sentences) — concrete observation or specific moment
2. Middle — the story or insight with evidence
3. Close — clean landing, no heavy CTA

## Vibe
- Operator-facing storytelling
- Credibility-building, quieter than X
- Less joke, more story
- Recruiting signals surface here more than X

## Avoid
- "Humbled to announce", "thrilled to share"
- Big inspirational conclusions
- "Agree?" closers
- Hashtag clouds, tagging dozens of people
